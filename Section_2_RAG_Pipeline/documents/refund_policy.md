# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 48 hours of delivery for orders
that arrived incorrect, incomplete, or damaged. To request a refund, open
the order in the app and tap "Report a problem," or contact support with
your order ID.

Refunds for orders that never arrived are processed automatically once
the delivery is confirmed lost, usually within 2-3 business days. Partial
refunds are issued when only some items in an order were missing or wrong;
the refund amount is limited to the price of the affected items, not the
full order total.

Refunds are not issued for orders that were delivered correctly but the
customer simply changed their mind after the food arrived, or for delays
caused by factors outside the courier's control such as severe weather or
road closures. If a customer cancels an order before it has been picked
up by the restaurant, they receive a full refund automatically. Orders
cancelled after the restaurant has started preparing the food are only
eligible for a partial refund covering the delivery fee.

Refunds are returned to the original payment method and typically appear
within 5-7 business days, though the exact timing depends on the
customer's bank.
