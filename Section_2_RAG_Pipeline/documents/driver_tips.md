# Driver Tips

Customers can add a tip for their courier either before placing the
order, as a percentage or fixed amount, or after delivery from the order
receipt screen. Tips go entirely to the courier and are not shared with
the restaurant or the platform.

Tips can be adjusted for up to 24 hours after a delivery is completed by
opening the order in "My Orders" and selecting "Edit tip." After 24
hours, the tip amount is finalized and can no longer be changed. If a
customer wants to increase a tip after that window, they can still do so
manually through their bank or card statement is not possible through
the app; instead they'd need to contact support, who can process a
supplemental payment to the courier in exceptional cases.

There is no obligation to tip, and couriers are paid a base delivery fee
regardless of whether a tip is left. That said, tips make up a
significant part of courier earnings, especially for longer or more
difficult deliveries, so many customers choose to tip more generously
during bad weather or for large, heavy orders.

Couriers cannot see the tip amount before accepting a delivery in most
regions, so tipping does not influence which orders a courier chooses to
take.
