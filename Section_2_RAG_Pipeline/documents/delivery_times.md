# Delivery Times

Standard delivery windows are 25-45 minutes from the time an order is
placed, depending on distance from the restaurant and how busy the
restaurant's kitchen is at that moment. During peak hours, typically
12:00-13:30 and 19:00-21:00 local time, delivery times can extend to
50-70 minutes because of higher order volume and limited courier
availability in a given area.

Weather is one of the most common causes of delay. Heavy rain, snow, or
extreme heat can slow couriers down for safety reasons and reduce the
number of couriers willing to accept deliveries, which increases wait
times across an entire city. The app automatically adds a weather delay
notice to the order tracking screen when this is happening, along with a
revised estimated time of arrival.

Customers can track their order in real time from the "My Orders" tab,
which shows the courier's live location once the order has been picked
up. If an order is running more than 20 minutes past its estimated
delivery time with no update, customers are encouraged to contact support
directly rather than waiting further, since this usually indicates an
issue with the courier assignment that needs manual intervention.

Restaurants that consistently exceed their quoted preparation time are
flagged internally and may be temporarily removed from search results
during peak hours until the issue is resolved.
