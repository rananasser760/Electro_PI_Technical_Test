# Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I track my order?**
Open the "My Orders" tab and select the active order. Once a courier has
picked it up, you'll see their live location on a map along with an
updated estimated arrival time.

**How do I contact support?**
Tap the help icon in the app, or select an order and choose "Report a
problem" to start a chat with support about that specific order. For
account-wide issues not tied to a single order, use the general "Contact
us" option in Account Settings.

**Why was my order cancelled automatically?**
Orders are cancelled automatically if a restaurant doesn't confirm the
order within 10 minutes of it being placed, usually because the
restaurant is closed or too busy to accept new orders. Customers are
notified immediately and refunded in full when this happens.

**Can I schedule an order for later?**
Yes, most restaurants support scheduled orders up to 5 days in advance.
Look for the "Schedule for later" option on the restaurant's menu page
before checking out.

**The app is crashing or won't load my orders. What should I do?**
Try closing and reopening the app first, then check for an app update in
your device's app store. If the problem continues, contact support with
your device type and app version so the issue can be investigated.
