Q: Can I still change a driver's tip after the delivery is done?
A: Yes, you can still change a driver's tip after the delivery is done, but only for up to 24 hours after the delivery is completed [2]. After 24 hours, the tip amount is finalized and can no longer be changed, although you can still increase the tip manually through your bank or card statement [2].
